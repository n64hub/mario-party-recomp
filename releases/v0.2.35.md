Fixes for Cast Aways, Bobsled Run, Desert Dash, Mushroom Mix-Up, Bumper Balls and Hammer Drop. Like v0.2.34 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `1159bd56730094bfc71be30113e1cfc8bacf34f3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.35-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.35-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- In Cast Aways in a window wider than 4:3, the chests and coin bags of the back row float in front of the near-left island as they leave on the left, instead of sinking into its sand.
- In Bobsled Run, the ice brick walls now stay whole as the sleds race past them, in both views. Before, at a high frame rate they split at their seams between the game's frames, with dark gaps and uneven heights, and snapped back.
- In Desert Dash, the archway's star and mushroom signs no longer have a thin dark line under them as the camera pans in.
- In Mushroom Mix-Up, the characters' feet stay on the center mushroom as the camera moves in at the start. Before, at a high refresh rate, their shoes vanished into the mushroom for a moment, several times in the first second.
- In Bumper Balls and Hammer Drop the sea now reaches the top of the screen. Before, a strip of the background (dark blue in Bumper Balls, black in Hammer Drop) showed above its far edge, which a TV's overscan used to hide.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions and rumble on a physical pad. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party).
