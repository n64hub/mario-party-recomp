A player's picture behind the launcher's menu, and seven fixes: Board Selection's preview pictures and its TV static, Shell Game's opening coins in a wide window, the cloud on a mini-game's rules screen, Skateboard Scamper's lava, Paddle Battle's river, and Coin Block Bash's items between frames. Like v0.2.29 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `1159bd56730094bfc71be30113e1cfc8bacf34f3`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.30-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioPartyRecompiled-v0.2.30-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioPartyRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**New:**

- The launcher's main menu has a picture behind it, made for the project by one of its players, with the title and the menu outlined so they read on it. It covers the window at any shape, cut at its sides in a narrower one.

**Fixed:**

- On the Board Selection screen, each board's preview picture is now one smooth picture. Before, a line ran across its middle, sharp in a big window, and its bottom-right quarter was drawn brighter than the rest.
- In Shell Game in a wide window, the coins poured from the top-left corner into the player at the start now fly whole through the window's left margin. Before, they were cut off along the 4:3 picture's left edge, and those past it not drawn.
- On a mini-game's rules screen, the soft cloud behind the title now fades out smoothly at its bottom. Before, a straight edge ran across it under the title, moving up and down with the cloud, plain in a big window.
- On the Board Selection screen, changing boards now goes straight from the board's picture to the TV static. Before, the TV went dark for one frame in between, a flicker each time.
- In Skateboard Scamper in a wide window of any shape but exactly 16:9 (16:10, 21:9, or a 4096x2160 one), the lava now flows on as one surface. Before, a grid of straight seams ran across it, where the lava's pattern broke off.
- In Paddle Battle, the river is now whole where its stretches meet. Before, a thin bright line ran straight across the water behind the raft, by the boulder in the middle of the river, plain in a big window.
- In Coin Block Bash, the coins, coin bags and stopwatches now keep their size, a player the hammer hits flies off smoothly, and the shadows stay round under what casts them. Before, between the game's frames, items flipped between tiny and huge, hit players flashed across the field, and a shadow could stretch into a thin line across the grass.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions and rumble on a physical pad. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-recomp/issues).
